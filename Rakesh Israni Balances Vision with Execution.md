# How Rakesh Israni Balances Vision with Execution

A clear vision has little value without consistent action to support it. Rakesh Israni, our founder, argues that while dreaming big is good – these big dreams remain mere aspirations unless backed up with consistent effort and execution. It requires us to bring as much if not more focus and intent to the day-to-day grind as much as the future prize, to make sustainable, lasting change.

"Planning is just the first phase; every plan requires ground work and execution, for [Rakesh Israni](https://isranirakesh.wordpress.com/), and his philosophy revolved around having feasible aims, staying systematic, taking responsibility and meticulously fulfilling each one of it as a smaller step that would eventually help to create it attain bigger ambitions without having them to turn out into the dreaded 'mountain of an obstacle'."

Another aspect of his philosophy is to remain adaptable. The professional path inevitably is fraught with unexpected twists and turns. Rakesh Israni is convinced that success relies on his ability to shift his direction and strategy without losing focus of the primary goal and remaining adaptable in any circumstances.

The second aspect that he emphasizes upon is regularity, as instead of long spurts of exertion, he prefers small persistent endeavors; and in order for discipline to have an enduring impact; consistency plays an extremely important role. If one manages to make gradual progress on any given basis, it would reinforce their conviction and develop expertise and ultimately yield the most beneficial results.

What [Rakesh Israni](https://www.filesure.in/director/gunjan-rakesh-israni-israni/00012493)'s methodology demonstrates is that there’s an effective synergy between vision and action. The path ahead is the objective you can build, but it only ever arrives when you work at building it consistently. That mindset serves us as a prompt for that persistent striving that actually translates to lasting success.
