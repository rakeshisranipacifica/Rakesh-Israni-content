# How Consistency Became a Key Part of Rakesh Israni’s Journey 

The story behind professional success doesn't usually begin with a single great achievement. Instead, professional success arises when you put in daily work, seek new experiences and persevere over time. Rakesh Israni shares a story of just that kind of professional growth.

Staying Focused Over the Years:

Business consistency is not about rigidity. This is about being consistent while staying flexible. This is the ability to be consistent while maintaining a flexible and agile approach in real estate, as the real estate market, expectations, and demand can change very quickly.

Business style: this will prove to be the dedication for you as he or she builds confidence with fellow workers, customers, customers and enterprise partners. It will also lead to opportunities and decisions that you will be able to learn from to become more mature in decision-making and preparing.

Turning Experience Into Progress:

Throughout the many years in the real estate industry, there are certain things that you may learn that you can never find in books or short-term jobs. Gain experience in the various elements of the real estate industry.

There is a lesson in [Rakesh Israni](https://github.com/rakeshisranipacifica)'s career journey on why grit and passion is still the essential qualities one needs in such a cut-throat industry. Instead of being attracted to instant gratification, a career path over the years can show actual working experience and allow people to be committed to their goals.

A Lesson for Aspiring Professionals:

The next conclusion to draw is that you will need to put in the hard yards. Determination, hard work, developing skills, learning to make good decisions and having the ability to adapt will all contribute to creating a strong professional foundation.

It can be super demotivating to be working on your career. But if you walk away from this message with just one thing, it's this: don't rush your way there. Everything is right: the greatest leaps forward are just about showing up, being a lifelong learner, and evolving along the way.
