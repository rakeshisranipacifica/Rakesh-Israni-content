# The Human Side of Business Leadership: Insights from Rakesh Israni 

Numbers might be a metric of success for most of the leaders, but we can't overlook people in this equation. In [Rakesh Israni](https://isranirakesh.wordpress.com/)'s view, great leadership is not just about commanding figures. It is more about connecting, knowing them, and cultivating an environment where each one of you is the best person. Your team has double-faced advantages when it strives with confidence with an intention to achieve.

A wise boss will always listen first before deciding. Every worker, client, and collaborator has their own angle that they may not have originally thought of or it was difficult for them to process. Listening thoughtfully and objectively gives you insight into their angle making solution building far easier in addition to creating positive business interactions among professionals. Additionally, an open discourse shows respect among all people inside the organisation.

However, there will be trials in each business endeavors. Great leaders do not panic when they are under pressure. They are confident in solutions and teamwork, and they move on without fear by being adaptable, learning their lessons. Businesses survive when leaders do.

Another essential component to making it will come from our constancy. Things, however small like keeping our word, being treated in terms of what is correct to a matter with clarity may come a long long way. While these actions may be relatively minor, but, may be the difference between a business owner of great regard or shame and the other.

But people always make things a legacy-especially because of the ‘humanity of leadership,’ that last, indelible footprint people make. And you see it in [Rakesh Israni](https://e27.co/user/rakesh.israni/)'s words again. With empathy, responsibility and doing the right things, a leader fosters resilient teams and resilient organisations that grow from strength to strength for ever.
