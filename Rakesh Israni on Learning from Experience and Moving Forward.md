# Rakesh Israni on Learning from Experience and Moving Forward

In many ways experience is the best possible instructor in the professional world. Each accomplishment holds an invaluable lesson while each struggle holds a deeper wisdom about what to replicate going forward and what not. The connection between experience and progress, according to [Rakesh Israni](https://about.me/rakeshisranipacifica), cannot be overlooked.

We can gain valuable experience from something more ordinary than a great accomplishment. By learning to think about everyday choices, conversations, problems, and mistakes, a business professional learns about what skills to play to and what areas to consider developing. By avoiding the trap of negative experiences simply being failures; they transform themselves into stepping stones.

Thinking outside of the box needs to happen from now on. As the business world continues to evolve there will be certain traditional approaches which you will eventually need to be ready to develop in order to come with. The capacity to think clearly in an always evolving workspace will enable individuals to succeed.

In [Rakesh Israni](https://substack.com/@rakeshisranipacifica)’s book, experiences matter, particularly if they can help shape future choices. The point isn’t just to remember, but to figure out why and do things better next time. That attitude, in his book, is the only thing that truly fosters professional growth.

One begins building a career with multiple experiences across time, looking at what happened, so that you know what it is going to happen, so that you can decide and grow.

This viewpoint of Rakesh Israni provides an important practical take on a crucial message – we can glean true benefits from the experience in the event the learning process made you adapt, grow, change, and move further.
