# Rakesh Israni's Take on Honest Communication in Business

“Honesty in conversation creates an opening to develop trust within any organisation,” states Rakesh Israni, as he puts the value of truthful conversation as one of the best base principles behind any success in business. “Honest and transparent communication with clients, business partners, and employees encourages trust while preventing unnecessary assumptions.”

In the opinion of Rakesh Israni, communication is not just the flow of information, but it is also learning. Allowing people to express what’s currently in their minds, so that you'll be able to make good decisions and co-work efficiently. Besides, if people know that they have been heard, they'll stay calm to brainstorm and reach common objectives together.

Being transparent becomes even more important when facing difficult situations. [Rakesh Israni](https://e27.co/user/rakesh.israni/) thinks that, instead of dodging challenges and problems, one should talk about them clearly. Discussing an issue openly helps people find reasonable solutions and continue their relationships with others. Accepting your mistake is also a sign of smartness.

"Good communication relies on two things - consistency and trust." - [Rakesh Israni](https://sites.google.com/view/isranirakesh) The professional success is not just the matter of hard work but it is the also the case of smart skills such as the use of appropriate language with clear intention not ambiguity, always follow their commitment, give update as committed, answer their question with honesty and not bluff or take unreasonable demand to get their things right to develop their credibility in business as well as at personal level."

Inexpensive and efficient techniques of forming a respected profession are to honestly present yourself to people you come in contact with at your current or future place of profession because people intuitively favour a truthful, polite, and reliable individual. Together, they eventually foster strong professional relationships, promote cooperation, and ensure a consistent course of professional development built on mutual reliance and trust.
