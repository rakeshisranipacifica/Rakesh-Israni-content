# What Today's Buyers Truly Appreciate – Rakesh Israni 

Buying a house now means something more than just finding a great address and pretty architecture. It is about finding a home that is well suited for the home buyers' daily lifestyles. They want practical homes that are livable and structured to fulfil their life goals. That’s why [Rakesh Israni](https://sites.google.com/view/isranirakesh) states that learning the expectations of these home buyers is what is essential to bring successful developments.

New buyers are demanding transparency throughout the process of buying a house. This comes down to good communication and sensible commitments, which in turn leads to developers selling what they advertise. Build that, trust starts to happen and that trust can turn around an entire transaction that sees individuals decide where they invest their money.

Another key concern when choosing a home is enduring quality of finish that stands the test of time past handover day. Good solid build, efficient layout, good natural light, helpful amenities, all combine to make everyday life a pleasure, and some of the key decisions now, may impact generations.

[Rakesh Israni](https://x.com/RakeshIsraniP), who believes consumers are buying more than physical walls and ceilings says, "they’re buying neighborhoods in which they can grow their families, meet friends, live and play – a place where convenience and quality life are prioritized alongside the house itself, and safety, green lungs and the basic necessities of life become paramount."

The real estate sector is evolving by leaps and bounds. Yet one aspect which continues to remain invariant: honesty, quality, and permanence. And by concentrating on these attributes alone, Rakesh Israni believes realtors can build homes and minds for this day and age and forever build and win trust.
