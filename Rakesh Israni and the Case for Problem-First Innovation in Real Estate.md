# Rakesh Israni and the Case for Problem-First Innovation in Real Estate

Most people think that the business of real estate has to be about properties, price and investment. But it has to be about human beings, and that perspective of [Rakesh Israni](https://substack.com/@rakeshisranipacifica) is praiseworthy for focusing on the core problems instead of the potential solutions. Studying the requirements of the buyers, investors, and even the community can assist the generation of a better or workable idea.

The problem-first principle works by observing and listening. It assumes we shouldn’t just jump at making guesses about what people really desire; instead, we ask what anxieties, needs, expectations, routines, constraints, and day-to-day challenges they may be grappling with. These would be similar for house hunters’ concerns, price limitations, needs to improve access, search for their lifetime dwelling, etc.

[Rakesh Israni](https://about.me/rakeshisranipacifica)'s views also go toward demonstrating why not everything that qualifies as innovative has to necessarily make use of a sophisticated piece of technology. An improvement, however modest in planning or delivery or the kind of communication you bring to service to the user, is just as valid a form of innovation provided it serves some need.

Also, it is critical to continue evolving and to be flexible to changes. Real estate is a dynamic industry that responds to client needs, neighbourhood growth, and technology advancements, economic fluctuations and market shifts. Approach to problem from different angles will help real estate professionals to find new avenues.

Problem-first thinking in a nutshell: It is the best and the only pragmatic thing I’ve heard regarding my ‘problem’-atics: real estate. Focus on researching the problem; the deeper one understands the problem, the more rationally one will make decisions, and the less one tends to run after superficial fads of ‘new and improved’. Make a problem worth solving – you get better ideas and experiences long term.
